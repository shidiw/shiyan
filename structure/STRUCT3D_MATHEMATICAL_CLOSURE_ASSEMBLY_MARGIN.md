# Struct3D Structural Assembly Energy Separation Margin

## Status

Frozen as a **conditional theorem contract** for the theory-compliant core.
The theorem does not alter the Stage 2D energy functional and does not infer a
positive margin from naming, deterministic ordering, semantic labels, or a
neural network.

## 1. Stage 2D energy

For a finite observation `X` and an explicit finite partition `P`, define

`e(A) = min_m [ F_X(A,m) + lambda_c k(m) ]`

and

`B_X(P) = cut_w(P) / total_w`.

The Stage 2D energy is

`E_X(P) = sum_{A in P} e(A) + lambda_b B_X(P)`.

The geometric model family, complexity functional, and weighted observation
graph are explicit mathematical inputs.

## 2. Partition quotient

Two explicit partitions are Unit-quotient equivalent when they differ only by
a permutation of their Unit ordering. The canonical implementation key is
`partition_quotient_key(P)` in `structure/theory_energy_model.py`.

Primitive labels are not part of the frozen Unit identity. Therefore the Unit
quotient key uses the Unit support and frozen attributes, not historical
primitive metadata.

The Stage 2D energy is well-defined on this quotient because both terms are
invariant under permutation of Unit ordering:

- `sum_A e(A)` is a commutative sum;
- `B_X(P)` depends only on whether two observation indices belong to the same
  Unit, not on the Unit's ordinal label.

Hence

`P1 ~_U P2  =>  E_X(P1) = E_X(P2)`.

## 3. Structural Assembly Separation Condition

Let `A(X)` be the explicit finite admissible partition family generated by the
observation-derived candidate mechanism. A positive margin `delta_E` is a
property that must be **verified** on this family.

The condition is

`|E_X(P1) - E_X(P2)| >= delta_E > 0`

for every pair of quotient-distinct candidates

`[P1] != [P2]` in `A(X)/~_U`.

This is the **Energy Separation Margin Condition**.

The implementation exposes:

- `Stage2DEnergy.separation_margin` — the requested positive threshold;
- `Stage2DEnergy.verify_separation_margin(...)` — finite-family witness;
- `Stage2DEnergy.require_separation_margin(...)` — release-gate assertion;
- `Stage2DEnergy.energy_gap(...)` — quotient-aware pairwise gap.

The implementation does not add a pairwise separation term to `E_X`. A
pairwise term would make the proof circular by manufacturing the property that
it is supposed to certify.

## 4. Assembly margin theorem

### Theorem

Let `A(X)` be finite. Assume the Stage 2D energy is finite on every candidate
and is well-defined on `A(X)/~_U`. If there exists `delta_E > 0` such that

`|E_X(P1) - E_X(P2)| >= delta_E`

for every quotient-distinct `P1,P2 in A(X)`, then every energy minimizer is
unique **in the quotient space** `A(X)/~_U`.

### Proof

Because `A(X)` is finite and non-empty, the set of finite scalar energies
`{E_X(P): P in A(X)}` has an attained minimum. Let `P*` be a minimizer.
Suppose there were another quotient-distinct minimizer `Q*`. Then

`E_X(P*) = E_X(Q*)`,

so

`|E_X(P*) - E_X(Q*)| = 0 < delta_E`,

contradicting the separation condition. Therefore no quotient-distinct
competitor can share the minimum. The minimizer is unique modulo Unit-order
relabeling. QED.

## 5. One-sided Stage 2G form

For a proposed minimizer `P*`, the equivalent release condition is the stronger
one-sided form

`E_X(Q) - E_X(P*) >= delta_E`

for every quotient-distinct competitor `Q`.

`structure/theory_uniqueness.py` accepts an explicit `margin` and an explicit
`equivalence` relation so Stage 2G can consume this exact condition without
confusing Unit-order relabeling with a distinct structural candidate.

## 6. Stage 2E connection

For Unit formation, an explicit competitor family `C_U` may be supplied and
`has_energy_margin(u, C_U, E, delta_U)` verifies

`E(c) - E(u) >= delta_U > 0`

for every distinct competitor `c`.

This is optional in the API (`energy_margin=0` preserves the earlier Stage 2E
contract) but becomes mandatory whenever the theorem-level positive margin is
claimed for a Unit formation step.

## 7. What is and is not proved

This theorem proves:

1. quotient well-definedness of the Stage 2D energy;
2. finite-family margin verification;
3. quotient-level uniqueness once the positive margin is actually established.

It does **not** prove that every raw observation automatically has a positive
margin. That stronger claim would require an independent lower-bound theorem
on the observation-derived admissible family and the actual Unit/boundary terms.
Such a lower bound cannot be obtained merely from non-negativity of the current
fit and boundary terms.
