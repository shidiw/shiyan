# Struct3D Hypothesis Elimination Contract

## 1. Objective

The final theory-facing path has no external upstream mathematical objects beyond
one finite observation

\[
X=(x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1})\in(\mathbb R^3)^n,
\qquad n\ge 2,
\]
with strictly positive diameter. The `n>=2` condition is required by the frozen
normalized boundary energy, which divides by the observation diameter.

The former boundaries are derived from the same observation context:

\[
X\to A_{\max}(X)\to\Gamma(X),M(X),G_B(X),N_X,S_X,C_R(X),\Phi_X.
\]

The canonical engineering implementation is
`structure/theory_observation.py` plus
`structure/theory_observation_pipeline.py`, and the final machine-checkable
certificate is `structure/theory_hypothesis_closure.py`.

## 2. Boundary elimination

### A(X) and Gamma(X)

\[
\Omega_X=\{0,\ldots,n-1\},\qquad
A_{\max}(X)=\Pi(\Omega_X).
\]

`Gamma(X)` is frozen to `A_max(X)` in the theory-facing finite core. Therefore
it is non-empty and finite, and every observation-index permutation maps the
family onto itself.

Implementation: `ObservationCandidateFamily` and
`observation_candidate_family`.

### M(X)

\[
M(X)=\{m_{point},m_{line},m_{plane}\}.
\]

The models are fitted deterministically from the coordinates of X. They do not
consume semantic labels, primitive labels, learned parameters, or caller
hyperparameters. Coordinate-axis ties are resolved by the fixed coordinate
index rule.

Implementation: `ObservationModelFamily`.

### G_B(X)

For every unordered pair of distinct observation indices,

\[
w_{ij}=\frac{1}{1+\|x_i-x_j\|/s(X)},
\]

where `s(X)` is the observation diameter. The boundary graph is therefore a
complete finite weighted graph for positive-diameter observations.

Implementation: `ObservationBoundaryGraph`.

### N_X and S_X

For a Unit candidate `u`, the neighborhood `N_X(u)` is generated by one-index
insertion/deletion moves inside `Omega_X`. The proper subcandidate family
`S_X(u)` contains every non-empty proper support subset.

Both are finite and deterministic functions of X and the Unit support.

Implementation: `observation_neighborhood` and
`observation_proper_subcandidates`.

### C_R(X)

The candidate relation domain is the complete ordered pair set of distinct
materialized Unit candidates:

\[
C_R(X)=\{(i,j):i\ne j\}.
\]

The actual relation set is then filtered by the observation-derived predicate
`Q_X` in `theory_relation_formation.py`. No external adjacency graph or
semantic relation labels are required.

### Phi_X

\[
\Phi_X:W_X\to\mathbb R^{23}
\]

is the deterministic coordinate map implemented by `phi_x`/`represent_observation`.
Its inputs are only the observation-derived context and the X-derived World.
The `ObservationRepresentationMap` wrapper makes the map itself a first-class
object rather than an external coordinate-function assumption.

## 3. Closed Stage 2D -> World -> Representation path

The canonical pipeline is:

\[
X\to\Gamma(X)\to E_X\to P_X^*\to U_X\to R_X\to W_X
\xrightarrow{\Phi_X}\mathbb R^{23}.
\]

`ObservationDerivedPipeline.select_partition()` performs the Stage-2D argmin
over the X-derived finite partition family. The World is constructed from that
selected partition and the X-derived relation formation rule. Representation is
then evaluated through the same context.

No external partition, model family, boundary graph, relation candidate set,
or representation extractor is needed on this path.

## 4. Final closure theorem

**Observation Hypothesis Elimination Theorem (implementation form).** For every
valid finite positive-diameter observation X, the canonical pipeline determines
all former upstream boundaries as functions of the single observation context.
Consequently Stage 2D, Unit formation, relation formation, World construction,
and `Phi_X` can be evaluated without any external mathematical object supplied
by the caller.

The theorem is an implementation-closure statement: it proves provenance and
finite construction under the frozen definitions. It does not assert that the
23-dimensional representation is globally injective or semantically complete.
Those are separate representation-theory questions.

## 5. Compatibility with legacy theorem APIs

Low-level explicit-input constructors remain available for generic mathematical
regression tests. They are not the canonical raw-observation theory boundary.
The release-facing observation path is the pipeline above.

This distinction is intentional: compatibility APIs do not become hidden
hypotheses of the Struct3D observation theorem merely because they remain
callable.

## 6. Regression contract

`tests/test_hypothesis_elimination_pipeline.py` verifies the individual boundary
contracts. `tests/test_hypothesis_closure_certificate.py` verifies the final
closure certificate:

1. `A_max(X)` is the finite complete partition lattice.
2. `Gamma(X)=A_max(X)`.
3. `M(X)` and `G_B(X)` are reconstructed from the same X.
4. `N_X`, `S_X`, and `C_R(X)` are generated from the X-derived context.
5. Stage 2D, World, and `Phi_X` share one `ObservationDerivedContext`.
6. World construction does not require an externally supplied partition.
7. Representation remains exactly 23-dimensional.
8. Observation-index relabeling preserves all closure cardinalities.

Passing these tests establishes the repository implementation contract for the
observation-derived boundary layer. It does not by itself prove universal
geometric semantics, global injectivity of `Phi_X`, or semantic completeness of
`D_R`.
