# Struct3D Observation-Derived Stage 2E Existence Theorem

## Theorem

Let `X=(x_0,...,x_{n-1})` be a valid finite non-empty Struct3D observation with
positive diameter. Let

`Omega_X={0,...,n-1}`,
`A_max(X)=Pi(Omega_X)`, and `Gamma(X)=A_max(X)`.

Let `U_X` be the observation-derived Unit family obtained by materializing all
blocks appearing in `Gamma(X)`. Then `U_X` is finite, non-empty, and contains
every non-empty support of `Omega_X` exactly once under the frozen Unit quotient.

Let `E_U,X(u)` be the Stage 2D observation-derived unit term. Assume only the
already-frozen Stage 2D requirement that `E_U,X(u)` is finite for every
`u in U_X`.

Then there exists a Unit `u* in U_X` such that

`Stable_X(u*) and MinimalStable_X(u*)`,

where `N_X` is the frozen one-point insertion/deletion neighborhood and `S_X`
is the frozen one-point-deletion-plus-singleton proper-subcandidate family.
Therefore `u*` is a legal observation-derived Struct3D Unit under the existing
Stage 2E contract with zero required external margin.

## Proof

1. `Omega_X` is finite and non-empty. Hence its partition lattice is finite and
   non-empty.

2. Because `Gamma(X)=A_max(X)`, every non-empty subset `S` of `Omega_X` occurs
   as a block of some partition: use `S` itself as one block and, if non-empty,
   `Omega_X\\S` as the remaining block. Thus the X-derived Unit family contains
   every non-empty support. Consequently every insertion/deletion alternative
   generated by `N_X(u)` also belongs to `U_X`.

3. `U_X` is finite and non-empty. The Stage 2D unit energy is finite on every
   member. Therefore the finite set

   `{E_U,X(u): u in U_X}`

   attains a minimum. Let `v` be any global minimizer.

4. Every member of `N_X(v)` is in `U_X`. Since `v` globally minimizes `E_U,X`,
   `E_U,X(v) <= E_U,X(w)` for every `w in N_X(v)`. Hence `Stable_X(v)` holds.
   Therefore the set of stable Units is finite and non-empty.

5. Choose `u*` among the stable Units with minimum support cardinality. Every
   member of `S_X(u*)` is a proper subcandidate and therefore has strictly
   smaller support cardinality than `u*`. Such a member cannot be stable by the
   choice of `u*`. Hence no stable proper subcandidate exists in `S_X(u*)`, so
   `MinimalStable_X(u*)` holds.

6. Thus `u*` satisfies `Stable_X(u*)` and `MinimalStable_X(u*)`. Stage 2E with
   the canonical zero required margin therefore materializes `u*`.

No semantic labels, neural network, external candidate family, external
neighborhood, external subcandidate family, or caller-selected energy margin
appears in the proof.

## Strict-margin corollary

Define the global pairwise Unit separation quantity

`Delta_U(X) = min { |E_U,X(u)-E_U,X(v)| : u,v in U_X, u != v }`.

Because `U_X` is finite, this minimum exists. Any energy tie forces
`Delta_U(X)=0`, so `Delta_U(X)>0` is exactly strict pairwise separation of the
entire Unit family.

However, `Delta_U(X)>0` alone does **not** prove that the zero-margin Stage 2E
witness is MinimalStable. The strict Stage 2E margin requires the stronger,
fully observation-derived witness condition

`Stable_X(u) and MinimalStable_X(u)`

and

`E_U,X(v)-E_U,X(u) >= delta > 0` for every distinct `v in U_X`.

The implementation searches the finite X-derived Unit family for such a
witness when `require_strict_margin=True`. Thus a positive strict margin is
never imported as a hyperparameter or hidden assumption.

If no such witness exists, the theorem still closes Stage 2E existence under
the canonical zero-margin contract; strict separation is correctly reported
as an observation-dependent property.

## Boundary status

This closes the **existence** boundary of Stage 2E. It does not claim that the
positive strict margin exists for every observation, nor does it upgrade
energy-minimality into semantic correctness. Those remain separate adequacy
claims.
