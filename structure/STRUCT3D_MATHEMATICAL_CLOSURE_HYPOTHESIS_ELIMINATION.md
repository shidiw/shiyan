# Struct3D Observation-Derived Boundary Closure Theorem

## Theorem

Let `X=(x_0,...,x_{n-1})` be a finite non-empty 3-D observation with finite,
nonzero diameter. Define the observation-derived boundary package

`B(X) = (A_max(X), Gamma(X), M(X), G_B(X), N_X, S_X, C_R(X), Phi_X)`.

The canonical Struct3D construction satisfies:

1. **Observation provenance:** every component of `B(X)` is a deterministic
   function of `X`; no semantic label, neural parameter, caller-supplied
   threshold, or external mathematical family is an input to the canonical
   path.
2. **Finiteness/non-emptiness:** `A_max(X)` and `Gamma(X)` are finite and
   non-empty; `M(X)` contains the frozen point/line/plane model universe;
   `G_B(X)`, `N_X`, `S_X`, and `C_R(X)` are finite; `Phi_X` has fixed dimension
   23 and is defined only on Worlds generated from the same context.
3. **Quotient commutation:** for every permutation `pi` of the observation
   indices, the relabeled observation `pi X` satisfies

   `B(pi X) = pi · B(X)`

   for every combinatorial boundary (`A_max`, `Gamma`, `N_X`, `S_X`, `C_R`),
   while the geometric model family and boundary weights are preserved as
   unordered geometric objects.
4. **World/representation compatibility:** if `W_X` is the canonical World
   generated from `X`, then relabeling the observation produces the relabeled
   World and

   `Phi_{pi X}(pi W_X) = Phi_X(W_X)`.

## Proof strategy

### A_max and Gamma

`A_max(X)` is the complete set-partition lattice of the finite index universe
`Omega_X={0,...,n-1}`. A permutation maps every block to another block and hence
acts bijectively on the lattice. Therefore the lattice is closed under the
permutation. `Gamma(X)` is frozen equal to `A_max(X)`, so it has the same
property.

### M(X)

`M(X)` is generated by the deterministic point, line, and plane constructions
from the coordinates of `X`. Reordering points does not change centroids,
axis variances, residual functions, complexity, or geometric signatures.
Therefore the model universe is unchanged as an unordered family.

### G_B(X)

Every boundary edge `(i,j)` is generated from the pairwise distance
`||x_i-x_j||` and the observation scale. Both are permutation invariant.
The edge weight is therefore preserved while its endpoint labels are mapped by
`pi`. Hence the weighted boundary graph commutes with the permutation.

### N_X and S_X

`N_X(A)` is generated by one-point insertion/deletion over the finite support
`A subset Omega_X`; `S_X(A)` is generated by all non-empty proper subsets of
`A`. A permutation is a bijection on `Omega_X`, so it maps the generated
families bijectively.

### C_R(X)

`C_R(X)` is the complete ordered pair domain over the X-derived Unit family.
The Unit supports are permuted bijectively, and therefore the ordered pair
indices are permuted bijectively as well.

### Phi_X

The canonical representation consumes the World together with the same
observation provenance. Its frozen 23 coordinates are structural counts and
statistics, not raw observation indices. Relabeling therefore changes only
representative indices, not the represented structural equivalence class.
The regression theorem verifies the commutation equality on the canonical
pipeline.

## Engineering consequence

The canonical pipeline is now strictly observation-facing:

`X -> B(X) -> E_X -> P*_X -> U_X -> C_R(X) -> Q_X -> W_X -> Phi_X`.

The former external boundaries are retained only as compatibility APIs where
needed by historical regression tests. They are not required by the release
path.
