## C. Observation-derived boundary definitions

For finite `X=(x_0,...,x_{n-1})`, let `Omega_X={0,...,n-1}`.

- `A_max(X)=Pi(Omega_X)` — complete finite partition lattice.
- `Gamma(X)=A_max(X)` — maximal theory family retained for theorem closure.
- `A_search(X) subseteq A_max(X)` — scalable finite search family generated by
  whole, singleton, and deterministic farthest-pair Voronoi partitions. New
  strategies enter only through the audited deterministic registry.
- `M(X)={point(X),line(X),plane(X)}` — finite canonical affine model universe.
- `M_X(A)=argmin_{m in M(X)} [F_X(A,m)+kappa(m)]` — local model quotient for one
  candidate Unit A. Thus Stage 2D's minimum over M(X) is exactly equivalent to
  evaluating the attained M_X(A) subset.
- `G_B(X)` — complete pair graph with `w_ij=1/(1+||x_i-x_j||/diam(X))`.
- `N_X(S)` — one-index insertion/deletion neighborhood.
- `S_X(S)` — all non-empty proper subsets of S.
- `C_R(X)` — all ordered pairs of distinct materialized Units.
- `Q_X(A,B)` — strong local geometric relation predicate: minimum cross-support
  distance is no larger than the median pairwise distance in `A union B`.
  This replaces the semantically weak rule “finite distance implies relation”.
- `Phi_X(W)` — fixed 23-D finite statistics of X and W, implemented by `phi_x`.

These objects are deterministic functions of X (and, where mathematically
necessary, the X-derived materialized world), finite in the declared regime,
and quotient-compatible under observation/Unit relabeling.

## D. Critical theory ↔ engineering boundaries

### X → A_max(X) → A_search(X)

`A_max(X)` remains the complete finite admissible space. The engineering search
path now uses the smaller `A_search(X)` so complexity is no longer Bell-number
sized in practical execution. The search family is a certified subset of
`A_max(X)`, non-empty for every non-empty finite X, and deterministic from X.

### M(X) → M_X(A)

The old global model universe is not itself a semantic commitment for every
Unit. For each candidate support A, `M_X(A)` retains exactly the models attaining
the local regularized minimum. Ties are preserved rather than broken by an
arbitrary semantic label. This is a finite, non-empty, quotient-compatible
selection because it is an argmin over the finite X-derived M(X).

### Stage 2D Energy

The Stage 2D functional is a **DERIVED EXTENSION**:

`E_X(P)=sum_A min_{m in M(X)}[F_X(A,m)+lambda_c kappa(m)] + lambda_b B_X(P)`.

Since `M_X(A)` is defined as the argmin subset of M(X), the unit term is
exactly the attained value on `M_X(A)`; no semantic information is introduced.
The observation-derived constructor consumes X-derived models and G_B(X).
The historical `structure/energy.py` remains regression-only.

### Q_X semantic adequacy

The previous predicate “finite normalized distance and positive confidence” was
formally observation-derived but semantically weak: every distinct Unit pair
satisfied it on finite X, producing a complete relation graph. The new Q_X uses
a local geometric scale derived from A union B, so relation formation can reject
geometrically separated candidate Units without any externally chosen threshold.

### Stage 2E stability/minimality

`N_X` and `S_X` are generated by the observation-derived context. The generic
Stage 2E functions remain explicit-input compatibility APIs, while the closed
path uses the derived neighborhoods.

### Unit → Relation → World → Phi_X

`C_R(X)` remains the explicit finite candidate-pair domain, while `Q_X` selects
the semantically adequate subset `R_Q(X)`. World construction copies that
observation-derived relation set. `Phi_X` then computes its 23 coordinates from X
and the resulting World without an external extractor.

## E. Preserved mathematical boundaries

1. `Object`, `Instance`, and `Hierarchy` must not be promoted into the frozen theory merely because an engineering representation exists.
2. `D_R=0` means equality of the supplied 23-D representations only.
3. Deterministic tie-breaking is not uniqueness.
4. A positive `delta_E` is verified on a finite family; it is never added as a circular Energy term.
5. Neural objectives **must not be reported as a proof** of latent metric equality.
6. The observation-derived model/relation definitions are explicitly marked as derived extensions, not historical recovery.
7. `A_search(X)` is a computational search subfamily, not a replacement theorem for the complete `A_max(X)` admissible space.
